# SEO in Jaipur — Backlink workflow (approval-first)

This process is designed for the existing site, https://seoinjaipur.in/, with a zero-paid-tools budget.

## Non-negotiable rule

No backlink is created, submitted, purchased, exchanged, or published automatically. The system may help discover an opportunity, assess it, prepare a personalised outreach draft, and track the outcome. Vishnu must approve each prospect and each final outreach message before it is sent. The publisher/editor controls whether a link is placed.

Do not use automated directory blasts, bulk guest-post submissions, paid link schemes, private blog networks, spun content, comment/forum spam, or keyword-stuffed anchor text. These can create search-policy risk and damage trust.

## Free discovery workflow

1. Find a relevant real business, association, chamber, local publication, supplier, client, event, or marketing publication using ordinary search and existing relationships.
2. Record the exact page where a link could naturally help readers, not just the site's homepage.
3. Review topical relevance, real audience, editorial standards, visible contact details, outbound-link patterns, and obvious spam signals.
4. Mark the record as Needs approval. Do not contact the site yet.
5. Prepare an individual email explaining the genuine value to that publisher's audience. Avoid demanding an exact-match anchor.
6. Vishnu reviews and approves the prospect and message.
7. Send manually from the business email. Record the date and response.
8. After publication, verify the link manually and record the live target URL, anchor text, link attribute (follow/nofollow/sponsored/UGC), and last checked date.
9. Recheck monthly and update the status if the link disappears or changes.

## Approval statuses

- New prospect
- Research needed
- Needs approval
- Approved to contact
- Contacted
- Follow-up due
- Accepted / in progress
- Live — verified
- Rejected
- Not relevant
- Link lost

## What counts as a good opportunity

- Relevant to SEO, digital marketing, Jaipur/Rajasthan business, or the publisher's genuine audience.
- A real organisation or publication with identifiable ownership and editorial purpose.
- The proposed mention is useful even if the link is nofollow.
- No payment or reciprocal link is required to manipulate rankings.
- The link points to the most useful existing page on seoinjaipur.in. Do not create new landing pages for this workflow.

## Anchor text

Prefer natural brand or URL mentions such as SEO in Jaipur or seoinjaipur.in. Descriptive phrases are acceptable when they fit the sentence. Do not repeat exact-match commercial keywords across many sites.

## Monthly review

Review the tracker for stale prospects, unanswered outreach, live links, lost links, and any suspicious links. Disavowal is not a routine task; investigate first and use Google's guidance before considering it.

## What the scheduled GitHub audit does

The weekly GitHub Actions workflow checks the public homepage response, title, description, canonical, robots meta, Open Graph title, JSON-LD presence, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml. It stores a report as a workflow artifact. It is read-only and does not change the website.

It does not claim to discover every backlink or read Google Search Console. Those require an authorised data source or manual review. No API keys or paid services are required for this first phase.
