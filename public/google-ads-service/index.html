Campaign structure and keyword intent

We organize campaigns around the services and locations you actually serve. Keyword research separates high-intent searches from research queries, while negative keywords help reduce irrelevant clicks. Ad groups and landing pages should stay closely aligned.

Ad copy and landing pages

Useful ads communicate the service, location and next step clearly. The landing page should answer likely customer questions, show accurate contact information and make it easy to call or send a WhatsApp enquiry. We review the mobile experience as well as desktop.

Conversion tracking before optimization

Before judging campaign performance, we define meaningful actions such as qualified form submissions, phone clicks and WhatsApp clicks. GA4 and Google Ads conversion setup depends on account access, consent and the website implementation. We avoid treating every page view as a lead.

Budget and transparent reporting

Spend, competition, search demand and landing-page quality all affect results. We review search terms, clicks, cost and recorded conversions so decisions are based on available data. Advertising cannot guarantee a fixed number of leads or sales.
