What we review first

We start by understanding your services, customers and website. The audit checks indexability, crawl paths, page titles, headings, internal links, mobile usability, page experience and existing organic search data. Priorities are based on what is actually holding the site back.

On-page and content improvements

Keyword research is mapped to real customer intent, not just search volume. Important service pages should explain who the service is for, what is included, where it is offered and how a customer can enquire. We improve page structure and connect related pages with useful internal links.

Technical and local SEO

Technical work can include sitemap and robots checks, canonical tags, redirects, structured data validation and performance improvements. For Jaipur businesses, local SEO also means accurate business details, relevant service-area information and a complete, policy-compliant Google Business Profile.

How progress is measured

We use available Google Search Console and analytics data to review impressions, clicks, relevant queries, landing pages and enquiry actions. Rankings and lead volume vary with competition, location, website quality and market demand; no fixed position or result is guaranteed.
