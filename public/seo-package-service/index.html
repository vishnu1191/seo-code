What affects the right scope

The work depends on website size, platform, current technical health, competition, service areas and how much useful content is already available. A small local business website needs a different plan from a large ecommerce catalogue.

Possible monthly deliverables

Depending on the agreed scope, work may include technical checks, keyword mapping, on-page updates, local SEO guidance, content briefs, internal linking and Search Console reporting. Deliverables should be written down before work starts.

A clear starting point

An initial review helps identify urgent technical issues, important service pages and realistic priorities. We explain what should be fixed first and what can wait, rather than adding tasks that do not support the business objective.

Honest expectations

SEO is ongoing and influenced by competition, demand, website changes and search engine systems. No package can guarantee a specific ranking, traffic level or number of leads. Reporting should show completed work and observed outcomes.
